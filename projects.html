A 240-key hotel in Riyadh relit end to end: lobby, ballroom, suites and the arrival court, run from one DALI-2 backbone.

01Brief

The owner wanted the hotel to feel warmer and calmer after dark without adding a single visible fitting to the restored ceilings. Every luminaire had to disappear into the architecture, and every space had to change mood at the push of one button.

02Approach

We specified Keros A spots with 18 mm recessed optics for the lobby, magnetic track for the restaurant so the chef can re-aim for events, and strip coves in the suites. A DALI-2 backbone ties 1,860 points into 36 scenes, with presence sensing in back-of-house corridors.

“Guests notice the light before they notice the room. That was the whole point.”

General Manager · sample quote

03Result

Energy use for lighting fell by 42% while illuminance on key surfaces doubled. The operations team now changes the ballroom from conference to wedding in under a minute, from a tablet.
