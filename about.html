C°LB is the lighting brand of Arak Lighting, founded in Riyadh in 1976. We design, supply and maintain architectural lighting, controls and smart-city infrastructure for the Kingdom.

1976Riyadh

Our story

It started with a shop in Riyadh and a simple promise: light that is specified properly, installed properly and looked after for years.

Arak Lighting opened in 1976, supplying fittings to the Kingdom’s first wave of hotels, ministries and family homes. Over five decades the company grew into a projects business, with application engineers, a controls team and field crews who commission what they sell.

C°LB is the name we now give that know-how. The degree sign is our reminder that light is measured: an angle, an aperture, a circle on the floor. Every product carries a datasheet, IES file and BIM family, and every project gets a named engineer.

Mission

To make every space in the Kingdom better lit, through precise products, honest specification and service that lasts as long as the luminaire.

Vision

To be the Saudi lighting brand that architects choose first, from a single spotlight to a smart street.
