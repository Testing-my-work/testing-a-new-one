Light is geometry.

01 · The idea

The ° in C°LB is not a symbol. It’s a shape.

  1. A circle

    The aperture. Every downlight, spot and uplight we make starts as a perfect round opening, machined to the tenth of a millimetre.

  2. An angle

    The beam. From a 10° pin spot to a 60° wash, the angle decides what is seen, what is felt and what stays in shadow.

  3. An orbit

    The system. Light, 5G, CCTV, sensors and screens orbit a single smart pole, run by KNX, DALI or DMX controls.
