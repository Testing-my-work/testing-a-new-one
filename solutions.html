Anatomy

Eight systems in orbit.

Every module docks into the same shaft and cabinet. Hover or tap a system to see where it lives on the pole.

System 01 / 08

Light

Asymmetric street optics with 0–10 V or DALI dimming. Lights rise when sensors see people and fall to 30% on empty roads.

  • Up to 160 lm/W
  • Type II / III optics
  • Zhaga socket
  1. Light
  2. 5G small cell
  3. CCTV
  4. Environmental sensors
  5. Digital signage
  6. EV charging
  7. Emergency call
  8. Public Wi-Fi

Use cases

Where they stand.

From giga-project boulevards to quiet residential loops, the system mix changes; the pole stays the same.

  • Boulevards & downtowns

    Light, 5G and CCTV every 30 m; screens at crossings for wayfinding and public notices.

    • 5G
    • CCTV

    Soft, glare-free light, emergency call points and EV charging at visitor bays.

    • Wi-Fi

How we deliver

From survey to switch-on.

  1. 01

    Survey

    Street audit, photometric study and a system mix per zone, delivered as a DIALux model.

  2. 02

    Configure

    Pick the design, height, arms, finish and modules. We issue drawings and a BOQ.

  3. 03

    Install

    Foundations, cabling and commissioning by our Riyadh field teams and partners.

  4. 04

    Operate

    One dashboard for light levels, faults, footfall and screens, with an SLA-backed service plan.

The range in numbers.

Heights
6, 8, 10, 12 m (sample)
Shaft
Hot-dip galvanised steel or aluminium, powder coated
Luminaire
40–200 W LED, Type II/III optics, 3000/4000 K
Controls
NEMA/Zhaga node, LoRaWAN or NB-IoT, DALI-2
Protection
IP66 head and cabinet, IK10 screen

Designed to 160 km/h (to confirm per site)
